# Product Template

Use this template for `docs/product.md`. Plain language, at most about two pages.

```md
---
date: [ISO date]
topic: "[Product name]"
tags: [product]
status: draft
---

# [Product name]

[One or two sentences: what it is and why it exists.]

## Problem

[What problem this solves. How people deal with it today.]

## What makes it special (USP)

[One sentence: the one thing that makes this better than what people use today.]

## Users

- **[User group]** — [what they want to get done]

## Main use cases

1. [A user does X to achieve Y]
2. ...

## First version

**In scope**
- [...]

**Out of scope** (deliberately not now)
- [...]

## Slices

The build order lives in `docs/slices.md`.

## Open questions

- [Question] — [why it matters]
```
